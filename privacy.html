Privacy Notice

This notice explains how RONDON handles information you submit through the AI Opportunity Assessment on rondon.digital.

Information you provide

The assessment may ask for your name, email address, company name, and a description of your business challenge. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive information through this form.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to review your enquiry, understand the business challenge you describe, and contact you about your request. Submitting the form does not guarantee that RONDON will accept an engagement.

Form processing

The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, and submissions may be forwarded to RONDON for follow-up. Formspree may process and store information under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review Formspree’s privacy policy for details about its handling of submissions.

Retention and sharing

We aim to retain enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to the request, maintain relevant business records, or meet applicable obligations. Access should be limited to people who need the information to handle the enquiry. We do not knowingly sell enquiry details.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the form. To ask a question about your submission or request correction or deletion where applicable, contact ronit@rondon.digital.

Contact

For privacy questions, email ronit@rondon.digital.
