Privacy Notice

This notice explains how RONDON handles information you submit through the AI Opportunity Assessment form on this website.

Information you provide

The form asks for your name, email address, company name, and optionally a description of your biggest business challenge. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to review your enquiry and contact you about your business challenge or potential RONDON services. We do not ask you to provide information that is unnecessary for this purpose.

Form processing

The form is submitted through Formspree, which processes the submission on our behalf and forwards it to RONDON. Your submission may be processed under Formspree’s own terms and privacy notice. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

Retention and requests

We retain enquiry information only as needed to respond, maintain relevant business records, and meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, contact ronit@rondon.digital.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if our enquiry process changes. The version published on this page is the current version.
