FLAGSHIP WORKFLOW / CONCEPTEnquiry-to-action coordination
Illustrative
A new enquiry enters through an available channel. The system identifies the event and captures the information it is permitted to access.
Available business rules, customer records, and relevant history are brought together where integrations and permissions allow.
The AI evaluates the available context against agreed instructions and criteria, then explains its assessment and uncertainty where relevant.
The system recommends an owner or follow-up. It initiates an action only when that capability is implemented, authorised, and within the agreed rules.
Where implemented, the workflow records its status, actions, and outcome so the team can review what happened and follow up.

Business & buying

What does RONDON do?
We help businesses use AI to reduce repetitive work, connect the tools they already use, and help teams decide what needs attention. We build the solution around the business problem—not a one-size-fits-all package.
Does RONDON work across industries?
Yes. We can work with businesses in different industries. We first learn how your business runs, what is getting in the way, and which tools you already use. Then we decide what kind of solution makes sense.
Do we need to replace our existing software?
Usually, the aim is to make your current tools work better together—not replace them. We check what each tool can connect to before confirming what is possible.
How is pricing determined?
Every project is different. After we understand your challenge and the work involved, we will agree on what to build, what is included, the timeline, and the price.
How do we get started?
Fill in the short form on this page and tell us what you want to improve. We will review your answers and discuss where AI may be useful. You do not need to know which solution you need yet.

Technology & implementation

What is the difference between automation and AI agents?
Automation follows steps that have been set in advance—for example, sending a confirmation after a form is submitted. An AI agent can look at the information available, work out what may need attention, and suggest or take an allowed next step. The right choice depends on the task.
Can RONDON connect our existing tools?
Often, yes. We check which tools you use and what they allow before planning the connection. If a direct connection is not possible, we will explain the options and limits.
How does implementation work?
First, we learn how your business works and agree on the problem to solve. Then we plan the solution, build and test it with you, and hand it over with clear instructions. Any ongoing support or further changes are agreed separately.
How does RONDON handle data and security?
We discuss what information the solution needs, who should be able to access it, and how it should be handled. The safeguards depend on the tools and the project. We will explain the planned approach clearly and will not claim certifications or protections we have not verified.
