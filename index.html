First demonstrated use case

For PR agencies, every promising enquiry deserves timely action.

When a lead arrives after hours, RONDON can use an agency’s target-client profile and qualification rules to prioritise the enquiry, explain why it matters, and recommend who should follow up.

The aim: reduce time spent manually triaging leads, keep follow-ups from slipping, and create more opportunities to convert enquiries into clients.
